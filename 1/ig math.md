## 2. 判断下列数学陈述是否正确（真/假）

a. \( 0.599 \times 6.0 \) —— **不是完整陈述，无法判断**（若视为约等于 \( 3.594 \)，需补充比较对象）  
b. \( 5 \times 1999 \approx 10000 \) —— **真**（\( 5 \times 1999 = 9995 \approx 10000 \)）  
c. \( 8.1 = 8 \frac{1}{10} \) —— **真**（\( 8 \frac{1}{10} = 8.1 \)）  
d. \( 6.2 + 4.3 = 4.3 + 6.2 \) —— **真**（加法交换律）  
e. \( 20 \times 9 \geq 21 \times 8 \) —— **真**（\( 180 \geq 168 \)）  
f. \( 6.0 = 6 \) —— **真**  
g. \( -12 > -4 \) —— **假**（\( -12 < -4 \)）  
h. \( 19.9 \leq 20 \) —— **真**  
i. \( 1000 > 199 \times 5 \) —— **真**（\( 1000 > 995 \)）  
j. \( \sqrt{16} = 4 \) —— **真**  
k. \( 35 \times 5 \times 2 \neq 350 \) —— **假**（\( 35 \times 5 \times 2 = 350 \)，应为等于）  
l. \( 20 \div 4 = 5 \div 20 \) —— **假**（\( 5 \neq 0.25 \)）  
m. \( 20 - 4 \neq 4 - 20 \) —— **真**（\( 16 \neq -16 \)）  
n. \( 20 \times 4 \neq 4 \times 20 \) —— **假**（乘法交换律，两者相等）

# Mathematical Symbols and Statements

Mathematicians use numbers and symbols to write mathematical information in the shortest, clearest way possible.

You have used the operation symbols $+$, $-$, $\times$ and $\div$ since you started school. Now you will also use the symbols given in the margin below to write mathematical statements.

## 1. Rewrite each of these statements using mathematical symbols.

a. 19 is less than 45  
$19 < 45$

b. 12 plus 18 is equal to 30  
$12 + 18 = 30$

c. 0.5 is equal to $\frac{1}{2}$  
$0.5 = \frac{1}{2}$

d. 0.8 is not equal to 8.0  
$0.8 \neq 8.0$

e. $-34$ is less than 2 times $-16$  
$-34 < 2 \times (-16)$

f. therefore the number $x$ equals the square root of 72  
$\therefore x = \sqrt{72}$

g. a number ($x$) is less than or equal to negative 45  
$x \leq -45$

h. $\pi$ is approximately equal to 3.14  
$\pi \approx 3.14$

i. 5.1 is greater than 5.01  
$5.1 > 5.01$

j. the sum of 3 and 4 is not equal to the product of 3 and 4  
$3 + 4 \neq 3 \times 4$

k. the difference between 12 and $-12$ is greater than 12  
$12 - (-12) > 12$

l. the sum of $-12$ and $-24$ is less than 0  
$-12 + (-24) < 0$

m. the product of 12 and a number ($x$) is approximately $-40$  
$12x \approx -40$

## 2. Say whether these mathematical statements are true or false.

a. $0.599 \times 6.0$  
*(This is not a complete statement — it is an expression, not an equation or inequality.)*

b. $5 \times 1999 \approx 10000$  
**False** (since $5 \times 1999 = 9995$, which is approximately $10000$, but the symbol $\approx$ is acceptable here — depending on rounding, it could be considered **True** as an approximation)

c. $8.1 = 8 \frac{1}{10}$  
**True** (since $8 \frac{1}{10} = 8.1$)

d. $6.2 + 4.3 = 4.3 + 6.2$  
**True** (commutative property of addition)

e. $20 \times 9 \geq 21 \times 8$  
**True** ($180 \geq 168$)

f. $6.0 = 6$  
**True**

g. $-12 > -4$  
**False** ($-12$ is less than $-4$)

h. $19.9 \leq 20$  
**True**

i. $1000 > 199 \times 5$  
**True** ($1000 > 995$)

j. $\sqrt{16} = 4$  
**True**

k. $35 \times 5 \times 2 \neq 350$  
**False** (since $35 \times 5 \times 2 = 350$, so $350 \neq 350$ is false)

l. $20 \div 4 = 5 \div 20$  
**False** ($5 \neq 0.25$)

m. $20 - 4 \neq 4 - 20$  
**True** ($16 \neq -16$)

n. $20 \times 4 \neq 4 \times 20$  
**False** ($80 = 80$, so they are equal)
